# Can another person implement this?

Work from the artifact and the sources its recipient can actually open. For a consequential handoff, ask the reader to walk one operation, including what to preserve if it fails or is interrupted. Have them name two different results the text would allow, or show where the text rules one out. A routine implementation choice left to the builder needs no clarification.

If an independent reading is requested or justified under the existing delegation policy, give a fresh reader only that material and the task. Omit the drafting chat, the author's preferred interpretation, and the expected answer. If the host shares history or the work stays inline, describe the check as an author walkthrough when reporting it. Disclose when a requested independent reading is unavailable.

The owner then compares the reading with accepted decisions. Restore an omitted decision at the point the reader needed it, or correct a stale link to an accessible source. Ask the user only when two plausible behaviors differ materially and the choice is still theirs. Recheck the affected example. Use the existing handoff; a second document or review round needs its own reason.
