# Evidence report

## Claim

State the exact behavior or property being evaluated and its scope.

## Expected outcome and authority

Identify the accepted user outcome, its source, representative example, preservation obligations, and any material expectation still unconfirmed. Reuse the working agreement; do not reconstruct intent from the implementation.

## Artifact identity

Record repository, revision, working state, build, environment, or other identity needed to establish freshness.

## Impact surface

List the material path, consumers, contracts, and preservation obligations considered.

## Evidence

Map each material expectation to its observation or gap. Record the target, command or action, boundary exercised, expected result, actual result, freshness, and one status: `PASSED`, `FAILED`, `BLOCKED`, or `NOT_RUN`.

Keep aggregate and subtarget results separate. A passing subtarget does not make its failing or blocked command or suite pass.

## Failures and gaps

List missing capabilities, contradictions, unreachable boundaries, stale evidence, and assumptions shared with the implementation. Separate outcome fit from technical correctness and unverified scope.

## Verdict

Choose `SUPPORTED`, `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, followed by one scope-bounded rationale.
