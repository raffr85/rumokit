# Change specification

## Intent

What outcome must change, for whom, and why?

## Non-goals

What remains outside this change?

## Current evidence

What existing behavior, contracts, and constraints were verified?

## Accepted decisions

Which product and technical choices are settled, by whom or on what authority? Which concrete user example anchors the result? Distinguish agreement from assumptions.

## Behavioral contract

Describe actors, inputs, outputs, states, transitions, invariants, and interfaces.

## Preservation and forbidden effects

What must remain true? What must never happen?

## Failure and compatibility behavior

Cover relevant errors, retries, concurrency, recovery, migration, and mixed-version behavior.

## Acceptance evidence

List representative examples and the authoritative observations that would demonstrate compliance.

## Open decisions

Record only material unresolved choices, their owner, and their consequence.
